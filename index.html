Luna

Your Windows AI voice assistant. Download the package and follow the install steps below.

Download Luna.zip

How to install

1. Download

Click Download Luna.zip and save it to your PC.

2. Extract

Right-click the ZIP → Extract All…. Do not run Luna directly from inside the ZIP.

3. Open the Luna folder

Open the extracted folder and run the included installer.

4. Finish setup

Follow the installer instructions. Luna does not require the UAC elevation step in this build.

5. Launch

Open Luna from the Start Menu shortcut. Right-click it and choose Pin to taskbar.

6. Voice apps

In Luna settings, open Voice Activated Apps to choose PC apps and assign custom phrases.

Minecraft Assist

The current package includes Minecraft Assist. Start it through Luna's voice controls, and Luna will assist with the Respawn flow while requiring confirmation for the action.
